# Korean Resources

Curated, high-trust sources for this workspace. Knowledge in lessons is drawn from
here rather than from parametric guessing.

## Knowledge

### Authoritative / primary

- [National Institute of Korean Language — 국립국어원](https://www.korean.go.kr/)
  The official language regulator of South Korea (est. 1991 by Presidential Decree
  13163). Publisher of the 1988 **Standard Pronunciation Rules (표준 발음법)**, which
  are the source of record for final-consonant neutralisation, cluster
  simplification, and every sound-change rule. Use for: settling any dispute about
  "correct" pronunciation or orthography.

- [표준 발음법 — full text (TUFS mirror)](https://www.tufs.ac.jp/ts/personal/choes/korean/nanboku/bareumbeop.html)
  Complete article-by-article text, readable without navigating the regulator's
  site. **Article 10** and **Article 11** are the definitive statement of the
  consonant-cluster rules and their exceptions (밟-, 넓-, verb-stem ㄺ).
  Use for: exact wording when a cluster behaves unexpectedly.

- ["Hunminjeongeum Phonetics (II)" — Journal of the Korean Society of Laryngology,
  Phoniatrics and Logopedics](https://www.koreascience.kr/article/JAKO202225852210743.page)
  Peer-reviewed phonetic and phoniatric assessment of the articulatory rationale
  behind the initial and final consonant letter shapes. Use for: verifying that a
  "the shape is your mouth" claim is actually in the 1446 design document rather
  than a learner-invented mnemonic.

### Reference

- [Wikipedia: Hangul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangul) ·
  [Hangul letter names](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangul_letter_names) ·
  [Hangul alphabetical order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangul_alphabetical_order)
  Well-cited and convenient for the full letter inventory, official letter names
  (including the three South Korean irregulars 기역/디귿/시옷), and ganada order.
  **Caveat, verified 2026-08-05:** the consonant table on the main Hangul article
  erroneously lists ㅉ among the 14 basic consonants. Cross-check inventory counts
  before trusting it.

- [Britannica: Hangul](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Hangul-Korean-alphabet)
  Editorially reviewed overview. Use for: historical framing and dates.

### Teaching material

- [How To Study Korean — Unit 0](https://www.howtostudykorean.com/unit0/unit-0-lesson-1/)
  **The best free Hangul course.** Six lessons covering every letter with audio for
  each one, plus unusually thorough explanations of *why* rather than just *what*.
  No paywall. Use for: audio models of the plain / aspirated / tense triples, which
  no written page can substitute for. This is the standing "primary source" recommendation.

- [Talk To Me In Korean](https://talktomeinkorean.com/)
  Well-produced and widely regarded as the best structured beginner path.
  **Note:** as of 2026 the actual lessons sit behind a paid subscription; the blog
  remains free. Use for: if/when the mission expands past script into grammar and
  a paid course is on the table.

## Wisdom (Communities)

Not yet raised with Kyle — these are candidates, not commitments.

- [r/Korean](https://www.reddit.com/r/Korean/)
  The main English-language Korean-learning community. Actively moderated, maintains
  curated beginner resource lists, and has a recurring Q&A thread. Use for:
  pronunciation sanity checks, "is this romanisation right", and resource vetting.
- [r/hangul](https://www.reddit.com/r/hangul/) — narrower, script-specific.
  Use for: handwriting and stroke-order feedback, which is hard to self-assess.
- **Highest-value option given the mission:** the Korean speakers already in Kyle's
  life. Reading a real message from someone who can correct him is a tighter
  feedback loop than any forum. Worth proposing once §6–§8 of the reference are
  covered and he can decode most of what he sees.

## Gaps

- **Audio is only the 19 Lab 01 consonant leads.** Isolated C+으 clips ship in
  `app/static/audio/consonants/` (see LICENSE there). Vowels, compounds, batchim,
  and native-speaker recordings are still the gap. How To Study Korean remains the
  best external model for those.
- **No handwriting/stroke-order source yet.** Kyle flagged handwriting as a
  secondary interest; nothing authoritative on stroke order is bookmarked.
- **No corpus of real beginner-readable text.** Loanwords carried Lesson 01, but a
  standing source of authentic short Korean text (signs, product labels, menus)
  would make the "read real text" half of every lesson easier to build.
