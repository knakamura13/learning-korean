Self-hosted Latin subsets (OFL)
================================

These woff2 files were subset from official OFL sources (Google Fonts mirrors
of upstream) by `app/scripts/vendor-latin-fonts.py`. Hangul remains
`NotoSansKR-subset.woff2`. The SIL Open Font License text is in OFL.txt.
