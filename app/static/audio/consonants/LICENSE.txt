Consonant lead clips
====================

These 19 lead clips (C + 으) plus mp3 siblings live in this folder.
ㅇ as a lead is silent, so `silent.opus` / `silent.mp3` is the placeholder
vowel with no consonant burst — the same fact Lab 01 and the review card teach.

They are original synthesis for this app, not recordings scraped from a
course or dictionary.

Lead `.opus` files are produced by `app/scripts/generate-consonant-audio.py`.
MP3 siblings, vowels, and finals are produced by
`app/scripts/generate-letter-audio.py` and must not overwrite existing lead
opus files.
