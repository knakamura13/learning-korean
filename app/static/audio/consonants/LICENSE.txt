Consonant lead clips
====================

These 19 files are short isolated Hangul initial consonants (C + 으).
ㅇ as a lead is silent, so `silent.opus` is the placeholder vowel with no
consonant burst — the same fact Lab 01 and the review card teach.

They are original synthesis for this app, not recordings scraped from a
course or dictionary.

- Copyright: learning-korean contributors
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Generator: `app/scripts/generate-consonant-audio.py`

Regenerate (requires Python 3 and ffmpeg with libopus):

    cd app
    python3 scripts/generate-consonant-audio.py
