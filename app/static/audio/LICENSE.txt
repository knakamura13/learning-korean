Letter audio clips
==================

These clips are original pedagogical synthesis for this app — not recordings
of a person, not runtime TTS, and not scraped from any course.

Inventory:
- consonants/  19 lead clips (C + 으), opus + mp3
- vowels/      21 vowel clips (ㅇ + V), opus + mp3
- finals/      7 unreleased 아+대표음 clips, opus + mp3

Merged vowels (ㅐ/ㅔ, ㅒ/ㅖ, ㅙ/ㅚ/ㅞ) share synthesis parameters so the
files are homophones; each jamo still has its own stable URL.

- Copyright: learning-korean contributors
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- Generator: `app/scripts/generate-letter-audio.py`

Lead `.opus` files come from `app/scripts/generate-consonant-audio.py` and must
not be overwritten when regenerating mp3 siblings.

Regenerate (requires Python 3 and ffmpeg with libopus and libmp3lame):

    cd app
    python3 scripts/generate-letter-audio.py
