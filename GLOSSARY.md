# Korean Script Glossary

The canonical vocabulary for this workspace. Lessons, drills, and learning records
all use these terms. Korean terms are given in Hangul with Revised Romanization.

**A term earns a place here once Kyle can use it correctly — not when it is first
introduced.** Entries below marked _(pending)_ have been taught but not yet

## The script

**Hangul** (한글, _hangeul_):
The Korean writing system, promulgated 1446. A featural alphabet — letter shapes
encode articulatory features, not arbitrary forms.
_Avoid_: Korean characters, Korean alphabet-syllabary

**Jamo** (자모, _jamo_):
An individual Hangul letter — one consonant or one vowel. The atomic unit.
_Avoid_: character, glyph, letter-piece

**Syllable block**:
The square cluster of jamo that Korean writes as one unit, one per spoken syllable.
Korean is written in blocks, not in a letter line.
_Avoid_: character, syllable character

**Featural alphabet**:
A writing system whose letter shapes systematically encode phonetic features. In
Hangul: base shape encodes place of articulation, added strokes encode aspiration,
doubling encodes tensing.
_Avoid_: phonetic alphabet, logical alphabet

## Positions in a block

**Batchim** (받침, _batchim_):
The final consonant slot at the bottom of a syllable block. 27 different letters and
clusters can fill it; they are pronounced as only 7 sounds.
_Avoid_: final, coda, bottom consonant

**Cluster** (겹받침, _gyeopbatchim_):
Two different consonants sharing the batchim slot. There are 11. Only one member is
pronounced before a consonant or a pause.
_Avoid_: double batchim, compound final — "double" is reserved for tense consonants

**Placeholder ㅇ**:
ㅇ occupying the initial slot of a syllable that begins with a vowel sound, where it
is silent. 아 is ㅇ + ㅏ and says only _a_. ㅇ is a real _ng_ sound only in batchim.
_Avoid_: silent circle, null consonant

## Manner of articulation

**Plain**:
The unmarked consonant series — ㄱ ㄷ ㅂ ㅅ ㅈ. Relaxed, with a small release of air.
_Avoid_: normal, simple, unaspirated

**Aspirated** (거센소리):
The series written with an added stroke — ㅋ ㅌ ㅍ ㅊ ㅎ. Same place of articulation
as the plain letter, produced with a strong puff of air.
_Avoid_: hard, strong, breathy

**Tense** (된소리):
The series written by doubling the letter — ㄲ ㄸ ㅃ ㅆ ㅉ. Tight throat, no puff of
air. The opposite extreme from aspirated, not a louder plain.
_Avoid_: double consonant (as a _sound_ description), stressed, emphatic.
In this workspace "doubled" describes the **writing**; "tense" describes the **sound**.

## Sound rules

**Representative sound** (대표음):
The one sound a batchim is actually pronounced as, after neutralization. All 27
possible finals reduce to seven: ㄱ ㄴ ㄷ ㄹ ㅁ ㅂ ㅇ.
_Avoid_: default sound, base sound

**Liaison** (연음):
A batchim moving into the next syllable when that syllable begins with the
placeholder ㅇ. 한국어 → [한구거]. Taught in Lab 06; batchim ㅇ does not jump
(already _ng_), and clusters split rather than sacrifice a letter (표준 발음법
Article 14).
_Avoid_: linking, carry-over, resyllabification (correct but heavier)

**Neutralization**:
The collapse of distinct written finals onto a single representative sound, making
words like 낫 / 낮 / 낯 homophones in isolation.
_Avoid_: reduction, simplification — "simplification" is reserved for clusters

**Morphophonemic spelling**:
Spelling that preserves the identity of a word part rather than transcribing the
sound. The reason Korean spelling and Korean speech diverge, and the reason the
sound rules are learnable rather than arbitrary.
_Avoid_: historical spelling, irregular spelling

**Tensification** (경음화):
A plain consonant hardening to its tense twin after a stop: 학교 → [학꾜],
식당 → [식땅]. Taught in Lab 07 (표준 발음법 Article 23). The stop does not
release; the following consonant absorbs the held pressure.
_Avoid_: fortition (correct but opaque), doubling (confuses with ㄲ-as-letter)

**Nasalization** (비음화):
A stop becoming its nasal counterpart before a nasal: 입니다 → [임니다],
국물 → [궁물]. Taught in Lab 07 (표준 발음법 Article 18). The mouth position
holds; only the airflow reroutes through the nose.
_Avoid_: assimilation alone (too broad — several rules assimilate)

- **Base shape** — one of the five consonants (ㄱ ㄴ ㅁ ㅅ ㅇ) drawn from the
  articulators, from which all 19 consonants derive.
- **Vowel harmony** (양성/음성) — the bright/dark split encoded by whether a vowel's
  tick sits above/right or below/left.
- **Iotation** — the second tick that adds a _y_ glide: ㅏ → ㅑ.
